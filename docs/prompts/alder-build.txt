Using the motion-pages skill, build a scroll-scrubbed process reveal ("ALDER"-class: a timber-cabin builder's site whose pinned hero raises a cabin as you scroll, then hands off to the business page) as ONE self-contained HTML file with NO WebGL, no libraries and no image assets — pure DOM plus one 2D canvas. Follow this spec exactly, then self-verify until it matches.

STRUCTURE
- A 500vh #track holds a position:sticky (top 0, 100vh / 100svh) #stage; the stage scrolls away on its own when the track ends. Progress p = scrollY / (track.offsetHeight − innerHeight), smoothed every frame (p += (target − p) × .1) so wheel notches read as a scrub. Body must never gain horizontal scroll width.
- Inside the stage: a full-bleed canvas scene, a top scrim (linear-gradient .72 → 0 over the top 46%) and a bottom scrim (.82 → 0 over the bottom 42%), a brand + nav header (links with an underline that draws on hover, a bone "Get a quote" pill), the headline "Scroll to raise your cabin." (light serif, clamp(46px, 6.2vw, 104px), one italic accent word), five stacked stage captions bottom-left (mono eyebrow "01 — The site" + one serif line each), a hint pill and a "01 / 05" tabular counter bottom-right, and a 2px progress bar with four stage ticks along the bottom.

THE SCENE (one function drawScene(ctx, w, h, p, t))
- Isometric projection on a 2D canvas: screen = [ox + (x − y)·.866k, oy + (x + y)·.5k − z·k]; k = min(w × .09, h × .062); the cabin sits right of centre on desktop, centred on portrait.
- Backdrop: sky gradient, a sun with a soft halo, four drifting cloud clusters (drift with t — the page is never still), two hill ridges with sine profiles, seeded pine silhouettes on the near ridge, a meadow ground with a warm light ramp, a lighter mown lot rhombus around the footprint, a soft radial shadow that grows with the build.
- Timeline as seg(p, a, b) windows, eased: survey stakes pop (.02–.09) and an orange string line draws around the perimeter via lineDash (.06–.16); the footprint darkens to soil (.14–.22); six concrete piers rise, staggered (.18–.25); the deck drops from +1.4 units on an ease-out (.28–.36); six posts extend (.36–.44), top plates run (.42–.50), five rafter pairs draw to the ridge and the ridge beam runs (.48–.56); the two visible walls rise with a clipped height and shingle lines (.56–.64); the gable fills (.62–.68); the roof lands from +1.6 units with standing-seam lines, a ridge cap and pale fascia (.66–.74); door, three windows, a gable window and a step cut in while the chimney climbs (.72–.78); day fades to dusk (.78–.92): the sun sinks, a moon and seventy twinkling stars appear, ground and cabin colours shift via mix(day, dusk, d); windows glow warm with radial blooms, a porch light comes on and smoke puffs rise from the chimney (.84–.97). Stakes and string fade out as the deck lands.
- Geometry: W 6 × D 4.2 units, deck top .72, eave 3.12, ridge 5.72 — the roof must be steeper than the view angle so the back slope is never visible over the ridge. Draw order: sky → stars/sun/moon → clouds → ridges → ground/lot/shadow → stakes/string/soil → piers (sorted back-to-front) → deck → frame lines → walls → gable → chimney → roof → openings → blooms → smoke.
- Captions: index = p < .17 ? 0 : p < .36 ? 1 : p < .56 ? 2 : p < .78 ? 3 : 4; toggle .on to crossfade (opacity + 14px rise on a soft-landing curve, .12s delay in); never swap text mid-transition. The bar scales with p; the hint pill leaves after target > .02.

THE HAND-OFF (the business page below the hero)
- Statement (eyebrow + light serif h2 with an italic moss accent + lead paragraph) → a dark ink stats strip (14 years / 210 homes / 40yr warranty / 4.9 rating; big light numerals, mono labels) → "Recent raisings": three figure cards whose 640×480 canvases are the SAME drawScene at p=.77, p=1 and p=.77 with a winter palette (snow ground, white roof, falling flakes) → three process steps with large numerals → a centred italic testimonial → a moss-green CTA band: copy + checklist on the left, a quote form (name, email, land select, site textarea, an ember "Request a site walk" button) on the right → footer. Section content enters with a soft-landing rise via IntersectionObserver, once.
- All easing from a defined cubic-bezier set, never browser defaults; hover states on every link, pill, card and button.

MODES

- Phone (≤760px): nav collapses to the pill, headline at 15% from the top, captions above the bar, hint hidden, stats 2×2, single-column grids and form.
