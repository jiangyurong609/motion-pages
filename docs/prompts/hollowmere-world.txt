Using the motion-pages skill, build a live game-world hero ("HOLLOWMERE"-class: an indie isometric action-RPG's landing page whose hero IS the game — a procedural Three.js world you can walk a knight through, with the game's HUD as the page chrome, a trailer shot from the same scene, and a wishlist close) as ONE self-contained HTML file with NO textures, NO models and NO image assets — every tree, rock, ruin and the character is code. Follow this spec exactly, then self-verify until it matches.

STRUCTURE
- A 100vh / 100svh #hero section (position:relative, overflow hidden) holding the WebGL canvas and an absolutely positioned #ui overlay (pointer-events:none on the layer, auto on children). Below it a normal <main> — the world does not need to be fixed; rendering pauses when the hero leaves the viewport.
- HUD chrome as DOM, ornate but restrained: a letterspaced serif wordmark + mono sub-line; a centred pill nav (The weald / Trailer / Press kit / Wishlist, the last one filled gold); a top-right clock "DAY 1 · 12:00 · DAYLIGHT" that advances with the world's time; a "Main quest" panel top-left (eyebrow, serif title, one line of copy, "0 / 3" counter with three pips); a circular minimap top-right (a 2D canvas drawn from the same object lists: road, trees, rocks, ruins, waystones, the knight as a facing triangle); a character card bottom-left; a vitality orb (radial gradient, gold ring, slow linear pulse ring) + a five-slot hotbar of inline-SVG ability icons with keycaps (Strike J, Guard K, Dodge Space, Nightfall L, Rest R) bottom-centre with a stamina line under it; a keys legend; two CTAs bottom-right ("Wishlist on Steam" filled, "Watch the trailer" ghost); a "Click the ground to walk" hint pill that leaves on first input ("Tap…" on touch).
- Panels: near-black glass (rgba .62, backdrop blur) with a gold 1px border, an inset second hairline, and four corner dots — the frame reads as game UI without any image. Colour set: ink #0b0e0a, bone #e9e2cf, gold #c9a961, moss #6f8a4c, ember #e0762e. No blue→purple anywhere.

THE WORLD (Three.js, one scene, orthographic isometric camera)
- Camera: OrthographicCamera at normalize(1, 1.32, 1) × 48 looking at the focus (≈ 43° elevation, 45° yaw); half-height 7.4 units on landscape, 9.5 on portrait; the focus lerps 35% toward the knight plus a small pointer parallax (fine pointers only). Renderer alpha:true, ACES tone mapping, NO shadow maps — shadows are one InstancedMesh of radial-gradient discs (baked canvas texture) under every tree, rock, pillar and the knight.
- Ground: a 44×44 plane (88 segs) displaced by 3-octave value noise with a flat clearing (smoothstep 3→11 units from centre), flat-shaded, VERTEX COLOURS: grass ↔ moss by noise, dirt along "the old road" (a 6-point polyline; distance-to-polyline < ~2 tints dirt, < ~.9 tints stone), ±10 % brightness noise so no two triangles match.
- Trees: ~64 placed in a ring r 8.5–19, rejected near the road, the ruins and each other; one instanced tapered trunk + FOUR instanced icosahedron canopy blobs per tree (one crown + three satellites, HSL hue .22–.30, per-instance colour, random rotation, 1.15× horizontal squash); ~18 % are bare — a taller trunk with three instanced branch cylinders. Ferns: 520 instanced 5-sided cones tilted randomly. Grass: 2,600 instanced 3-sided cones (r .03, h .22) with darker per-instance greens. Rocks: ONE icosahedron with jittered vertices, 34 instances with non-uniform scale (x/z 0.9–1.5, y 0.6–0.9) and low-saturation brown-grey. Flagstones: 48 instanced thin boxes along the road, jittered, low-value stone.
- Ruins (at −6, −6): five box pillars with small tilts (two moss-tinted), a half-torus arch on two of them, a fallen cylinder column, an altar slab. Three waystones: box + 4-sided cone cap + a small sphere lamp (dark MeshBasic) with a PointLight at 0 intensity until lit.
- Campfire: nine instanced dodecahedron stones in a ring, three crossed log cylinders, TWO crossed additive planes with a canvas flame gradient (scale/position jitter from sines of t), 48 embers as Points (canvas radial sprite, sizeAttenuation:false so they survive the orthographic camera) whose positions are a pure function of t, a warm PointLight whose base intensity comes from time-of-day and flickers by sines of t (never multiply the light by itself across frames).
- The wayfarer: a Group of primitives — two leg pivots (capsule + boot box) at the hips, a capsule torso, torus belt, two sphere pauldrons, a sphere head with a dark visor box and a gold crest fin, a right-arm pivot carrying the sword (box blade + gold crossguard), a left-arm pivot carrying a squashed-sphere shield with a torus boss, a cloak plane hinged at the neck. Steel = MeshStandard colour ~#9aa4ad, metalness .3, roughness .5 (high metalness with no environment map renders BLACK). Walk cycle: legs ±.7 rad and arms counter-swing from sin(t×9.5) scaled by a walk blend that ramps in/out; a small body bob; the cloak leans with speed.
- Time of day: tod 0–1 cycling every ~64 s (day counter increments on wrap). Sun colour/intensity, hemisphere sky/ground colours, exposure and fire/waystone light intensities all lerp from a daylight weight d = smoothstep(.02,.14,tod)·(1−smoothstep(.5,.66,tod)) and a dusk gaussian around .56. Night must still read: sun ≥ .55, hemisphere ≥ .75, exposure ≥ .88, fire radius 13. The hero's CSS background swaps to the night ink when d < .5.

PLAY
- Click/tap the ground: raycast the pointer onto the y=0 plane, clamp to the clearing radius (9.5 landscape / 3.8 portrait), set the target, show a pulsing ring marker there. The knight turns toward the target (shortest-angle lerp) and walks at 2.3 u/s; the ring fades on arrival.
- Waystones light when the knight comes within 1.5 units (3.6 on portrait, where they sit off-screen): lamp turns gold, its PointLight comes on, the quest counter and pips update; at 3/3 a "Quest complete" panel toasts with a wishlist link.
- Hotbar + keys: Strike swings the sword arm (−2.3 rad over .5 s), Guard raises the shield (.9 s), Dodge dashes 9 u/s for .32 s and dents the stamina line (refills from t), Nightfall jumps tod to .6 (or back to .2), Rest walks the knight to the fire. Every slot lifts and glows on hover and flashes on use.
- Autopilot: if nobody has clicked for 5 s, the knight walks to the next unlit waystone himself, then wanders — the hero is never still, and the quest completes even for a passive viewer.
- Trailer ("Watch the trailer" and the play disc in the page): the SAME scene through a PerspectiveCamera in three 4 s shots — a low orbit around the knight at the fire, a dolly toward the arch, a crane up over the weald that lands on a centred serif title card with a chipped subtitle; letterbox bars (11vh) slide in, HUD fades, an Exit button and Esc leave. The knight is sent to the fire first.

THE PAGE BELOW
- Statement (eyebrow + light serif h2 with an italic gold word + lead) beside three stat chips ("0 textures", "1 HTML file", "~40 KB of world code") → "Screenshots" grid of three figures whose canvases are rendered ONCE by the same renderer from other cameras at other hours (campfire at dusk, the arch by day, the weald by night with every waystone lit) → three feature columns (Procedural / Playable hero / Trailer) → the trailer frame (a 16:9 canvas poster from the crane camera + a frosted play disc with a linear ripple ring + meta line) → a wishlist band (copy + checklist, a form with email + platform select + gold button; submit shows a toast) → footer. Section content rises in via IntersectionObserver, once.
- Easing from a defined set (--e expo-out for entrances/hover, --ei ease-in for leaving, --em for the bars, --el for the disc bounce); loops linear. :focus-visible gold outline. Every interactive element has a hover transition.

MODES
- ?still: t frozen, tod .47, the first waystone lit, the knight standing at the fire, no autopilot, no cinematic, entrances pre-settled. ?tod=0..1 pins the hour for screenshots (day .22, dusk .56, night .8). prefers-reduced-motion behaves as still.
- Phone (≤760px): nav hidden, wordmark smaller, clock drops the day, quest panel full-width without copy, minimap and character card hidden, hotbar shrinks to icons, CTAs become a two-up row; the walk radius and camera framing shrink so the knight never leaves the frame.
