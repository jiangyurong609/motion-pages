Using the motion-pages skill, build a live game-world hero ("HOLLOWMERE"-class: an indie isometric action-RPG's landing page whose hero IS the game — a procedural Three.js world you can walk a knight through, with the game's HUD as the page chrome, a trailer shot from the same scene, and a wishlist close) as ONE self-contained HTML file with NO image assets, NO models and NO texture files — every texture is painted on a canvas at load, every tree, rock, ruin and the character is code, and the target is the fidelity of a real isometric ARPG (soft shadows, leafy canopies, swaying grass, a pond, pollen in the light), not a low-poly toy. Follow this spec exactly, then self-verify until it matches.

STRUCTURE
- A 100vh / 100svh #hero section (position:relative, overflow hidden) holding the WebGL canvas and an absolutely positioned #ui overlay (pointer-events:none on the layer, auto on children). Below it a normal <main> — the world does not need to be fixed; rendering pauses when the hero leaves the viewport.
- HUD chrome as DOM, ornate but restrained: a letterspaced serif wordmark + mono sub-line; a centred pill nav (The weald / Trailer / Press kit / Wishlist, the last one filled gold); a top-right clock "DAY 1 · 12:00 · DAYLIGHT" that advances with the world's time; a "Main quest" panel top-left (eyebrow, serif title, one line of copy, "0 / 3" counter with three pips); a circular minimap top-right (a 2D canvas drawn from the same object lists: road, trees, rocks, ruins, waystones, the knight as a facing triangle); a character card bottom-left; a vitality orb (radial gradient, gold ring, slow linear pulse ring) + a five-slot hotbar of inline-SVG ability icons with keycaps (Strike J, Guard K, Dodge Space, Nightfall L, Rest R) bottom-centre with a stamina line under it; a keys legend; two CTAs bottom-right ("Wishlist on Steam" filled, "Watch the trailer" ghost); a "Click the ground to walk" hint pill that leaves on first input ("Tap…" on touch).
- Panels: near-black glass (rgba .8, backdrop blur — .62 fails contrast over a bright forest) with a gold 1px border, an inset second hairline, and four corner dots — the frame reads as game UI without any image. Colour set: ink #0b0e0a, bone #e9e2cf, gold #c9a961, moss #6f8a4c, ember #e0762e. No blue→purple anywhere.

THE WORLD (Three.js, one scene, tilted perspective camera)
- Camera: PerspectiveCamera fov 30 (44 on portrait) at normalize(1, 1.28, 1) × 44 looking at a focus that lerps 35 % toward the knight plus a small pointer parallax (fine pointers only). Renderer alpha:true, ACES tone mapping, ONE shadow-casting directional light (PCFSoft, 2048 map, ortho frustum ±19 units that follows the focus, bias −.0007, normalBias .04), a hemisphere light, and an image-based environment: build a PMREM from a vertex-coloured gradient sky sphere + a bright sun ball (scene.environmentIntensity .55 by day) — this is what makes steel read as steel. Fog 50→84 (the camera focus is 44 away — fog that starts nearer washes the whole clearing).
- Procedural textures, all painted on canvases at load with periodic value noise (a lattice modulo so tiles wrap) and fbm: a 768² ground albedo (grass↔moss by noise, leaf-litter and dry-blade speckles, the road baked in via distance-to-polyline with a noise-wobbled edge, mud around the pond) + a tiled bump map; bark (streaks + dark seams) + bump; stone (noise + moss tint + stroked cracks) + bump; a water normal map from finite differences of noise; alpha cards for leaf clusters (46 gradient ellipses with midribs), grass blades (14 tapered curves), a fern frond (stem + leaflet pairs) and flowers; a soft sprite and a flame gradient.
- Ground: a 44×44 plane (120 segs) displaced by noise with a flat clearing (smoothstep 3→11 units) and a pond depression at (−9.5, 7.5) r 3.6; the water is a CircleGeometry with the scrolling normal map, roughness .1, opacity .86, envMapIntensity 1.3.
- Trees: ~50 in a ring r 11–21, none in the quadrant in front of the camera (x+z > 13), rejected near the road, ruins, pond and each other; instanced tapered trunks (noise-jittered cylinder, bark map + bump) with three instanced branch cylinders; canopies = 34 instanced leaf-card planes per tree (22 on portrait/lite) in a flattened ball (cbrt(rand) radius), random Euler rotation, per-instance HSL darkening toward the underside; alphaTest .5, DoubleSide, castShadow with a customDepthMaterial (MeshDepthMaterial + map + alphaTest). ~15 % are bare (taller trunk, branches, no cards).
- Undergrowth: 5,600 instanced grass-blade cards (2,600 on portrait), 380 ferns × 3 crossed cards, 220 flowers × 2 cards, all avoiding the road and pond. Wind: one onBeforeCompile hook on those materials that reads the instance origin and adds sin(uTime·1.7 + ip.x·.6 + ip.z·.8) displacement scaled by uv.y² (amplitude .16 grass, .07 fern, .05 leaves); uTime frozen in still mode.
- Rocks: three noise-displaced icosahedra (detail 3), ten instances each, non-uniform scale (x/z .9–1.6, y .6–.95), low-value stone colours (HSL L .38–.62). Flagstones: 40 instanced jittered thin boxes along the road, stone material.
- Ruins (at −6, −6): five noise-jittered box pillars with small tilts (two moss-tinted), a half-torus arch on two of them, a fallen cylinder column, an altar slab — stone map + bump, cast and receive. Three waystones: box + 4-sided cone cap + a small sphere lamp (emissive when lit) with a PointLight at 0 until lit.
- Campfire: nine instanced dodecahedron stones in a ring, three crossed bark logs, TWO crossed additive flame planes (scale/position jitter from sines of t), 48 embers as Points (soft sprite, size .45) whose positions are a pure function of t, a warm PointLight (radius 14) whose base intensity comes from time-of-day and flickers by sines of t (never multiply the light by itself across frames). 320 pollen points drift through the clearing (additive, faded by daylight). Light shafts: a DOM overlay of a repeating linear gradient at 112°, mix-blend-mode screen, radial-masked to the sun corner, drifting on a linear loop, opacity tied to daylight.
- The wayfarer (scale 1.25): ~25 primitives on pivots — two leg pivots (mail capsule + steel greave + leather boot), a mail skirt cone, a capsule torso with a breastplate sphere, a leather belt torus, two sphere pauldrons, a dark head inside a steel helmet sphere with a visor bar and a gold crest, a right-arm pivot carrying a gauntlet and a tapered cylinder blade scaled flat with a gold crossguard and pommel, a left-arm pivot carrying a squashed-sphere shield with a torus boss, a cloak plane hinged at the neck. Steel = MeshStandard #aeb4ba, metalness .88, roughness .36, envMapIntensity 1.2 (only works with the environment map). Walk cycle: legs ±.7 rad and arms counter-swing from sin(t×9.5) scaled by a walk blend that ramps in/out; a small body bob; the cloak leans with speed. Everything casts and receives shadows.
- Time of day: tod 0–1 cycling every ~64 s (day counter increments on wrap). Sun colour/intensity, hemisphere sky/ground colours, fog colour, environment intensity, exposure and fire/waystone light intensities all lerp from a daylight weight d = smoothstep(.02,.14,tod)·(1−smoothstep(.5,.66,tod)) and a dusk gaussian around .56. Night must still read: sun ≥ .9, hemisphere ≥ .75, exposure ≥ .95, environment ≥ .18. The hero's CSS background swaps to the night ink when d < .5.

PLAY
- Click/tap the ground: raycast the pointer onto the y=0 plane, clamp to the clearing radius (9.5 landscape; on portrait derive it from the visible width) and keep the knight out of the pond, set the target, show a pulsing ring marker there. The knight turns toward the target (shortest-angle lerp) and walks at 2.3 u/s; the ring fades on arrival.
- Waystones light when the knight comes within 1.5 units (3.6 on portrait, where they sit off-screen): lamp turns gold, its PointLight comes on, the quest counter and pips update; at 3/3 a "Quest complete" panel toasts with a wishlist link.
- Hotbar + keys: Strike swings the sword arm (−2.3 rad over .5 s), Guard raises the shield (.9 s), Dodge dashes 9 u/s for .32 s and dents the stamina line (refills from t), Nightfall jumps tod to .6 (or back to .2), Rest walks the knight to the fire. Every slot lifts and glows on hover and flashes on use.
- Autopilot: if nobody has clicked for 5 s, the knight walks to the next unlit waystone himself, then wanders — the hero is never still, and the quest completes even for a passive viewer.
- Trailer ("Watch the trailer" and the play disc in the page): the SAME scene through a PerspectiveCamera in three 4 s shots — a low orbit around the knight at the fire, a dolly toward the arch, a crane up over the weald that lands on a centred serif title card with a chipped subtitle; letterbox bars (11vh) slide in, HUD fades, an Exit button and Esc leave. The knight is sent to the fire first and tod is pinned to .5 for the duration.

THE PAGE BELOW
- Statement (eyebrow + light serif h2 with an italic gold word + lead) beside three stat chips ("0 image requests — every texture is painted at load", "1 HTML file", "~60 KB of world code") → "Screenshots" grid of three figures whose canvases are rendered ONCE by the same renderer from other cameras at other hours (campfire at dusk, the arch by day, the weald by night with every waystone lit) → three feature columns (Procedural / Playable hero / Trailer) → the trailer frame (a 16:9 canvas poster from the crane camera + a frosted play disc with a linear ripple ring + meta line) → a wishlist band (copy + checklist, a form with email + platform select + gold button; submit shows a toast) → footer. Section content rises in via IntersectionObserver, once.
- Easing from a defined set (--e expo-out for entrances/hover, --ei ease-in for leaving, --em for the bars, --el for the disc bounce); loops linear. :focus-visible gold outline. Every interactive element has a hover transition.

MODES
- ?still: t frozen, tod .47, the first waystone lit, the knight standing at the fire, no autopilot, no cinematic, entrances pre-settled. ?tod=0..1 pins the hour for screenshots (day .22, dusk .56, night .8). ?lite halves the ground texture, shadow map, grass and canopy counts. prefers-reduced-motion behaves as still.
- Phone (≤760px): nav hidden, wordmark smaller, clock drops the day, quest panel full-width without copy, minimap and character card hidden, hotbar shrinks to icons, CTAs become a two-up row; the walk radius and camera framing shrink so the knight never leaves the frame.
